MetaVariant Privacy Notice

MetaVariant (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy. This notice explains what data we collect when you use the Product Variations generator, why we collect it, and the options you have to control your information.

1. Data We Process

We collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Account information – name, email address, authentication tokens, and referral codes provided during registration or sign-in.
  • Usage data – prompts you create, images you upload or generate, coin balances, and session metadata needed to deliver the service.
  • Device & analytics data – anonymised logs about feature engagement, crash diagnostics, and performance collected via optional cookies (enabled only with your consent).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate you and keep your workspace synced across devices.
  • Generate AI product variations, descriptions, and session history on your behalf.
  • Monitor feature usage to improve prompt packs and stability (analytics cookies are optional).
  • Send transactional messages (e.g., coin purchases, security alerts). Marketing messages are opt-in only.

3. Data Sharing & Storage

We use trusted processors to host infrastructure, deliver email, and process payments (Stripe). Data is stored in the European Union or the United States with safeguards that meet GDPR and industry certifications. We never sell your personal information.

4. Your Rights

You can request to:

  • Access, update, or delete personal data we hold about you.
  • Export a copy of your generated sessions.
  • Withdraw consent for analytics/marketing cookies at any time via the GDPR button in the app.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

5. Contact

Email privacy@metavariants.com for any privacy questions or requests. We respond within 30 days.
