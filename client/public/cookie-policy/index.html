MetaVariant Cookie Policy

This policy explains how MetaVariant (“we”) uses cookies and similar technologies in the Product Variations creator. We only place optional cookies when you grant consent inside the app’s GDPR preferences panel.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, and help us improve the service. We also use localStorage for similar purposes. You can manage your preferences anytime with the “GDPR & Cookies” floating button inside the app.

2. Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Examples & Retention
Essential Required to run the service, enforce security, and store your cookie preferences. Session tokens (expires on logout), workspace cache (30 days), cookie preference record (12 months). These cannot be disabled.
Analytics (optional) Measure feature adoption, generator performance, and anonymised usage trends. Privacy-friendly analytics cookie (expires after 13 months). Only set when you opt-in via the GDPR panel.
Marketing (optional) Remember that you opted to receive product updates or campaigns. Marketing opt-in flag (expires after 12 months). Set only when you explicitly consent.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies in several ways:

  • Use the in-app GDPR button to accept all, reject non-essential cookies, or fine-tune analytics/marketing preferences.
  • Clear cookies in your browser settings to remove stored identifiers.
  • Block or delete cookies at the browser level (note: essential functionality like login may stop working).

4. Updates

We may update this policy when we introduce new features or external services. Any significant changes will be announced in the app, and you may be asked to review your preferences again.

5. Contact

If you have questions about our cookie practices, email privacy@metavariants.com.
